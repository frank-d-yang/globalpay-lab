# Out of Scope

The following items are excluded from the 16-week MVP:

- Real money movement
- Real card storage or processing
- Real Apple Pay or Google Pay integration
- Real bank-account connectivity
- PCI DSS certification
- Real KYC or AML vendor integration
- Cryptocurrency or blockchain
- Real foreign-exchange execution
- Quantitative trading
- Native mobile application
- Machine-learning fraud decisions
- Production-grade multi-region deployment
- Multi-cloud infrastructure
- Large microservice architecture
- Full ERP integration
- Consumer lending
- Insurance products

## Change-control rule

A new idea may enter the MVP only when it directly replaces an existing
capability or is required to make an existing capability correct.
All other ideas go to the Future Backlog.
